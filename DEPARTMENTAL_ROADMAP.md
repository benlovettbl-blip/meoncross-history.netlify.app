# The History Department: Master Pedagogical Roadmap & Implementation Ledger

> **Commercial Independence & Neutrality:** An official, institutional-neutral departmental ledger for tracking the rollout of High-Challenge Disciplinary Literacy, the 2-Lesson Enquiry Choreography, 2-Page Pupil Workbooks, and Schemes of Work across all 16 curriculum units.

---

## 🧭 Executive Vision & Core Standards
1. **The High-Challenge, Decoded Classroom:**
   We never truncate or dumb down historical texts (calibrated at reading ages 16–18+). Instead, we eliminate decoding friction through teacher-modelled whole-class shared reading, assistive speech prosody tracking, and isolated lexical pre-teaching.
2. **The 2-Lesson Enquiry Choreography (50 mins + 50 mins):**
   - **Lesson 1:** Immersion, Historical Outpost, Whole-Class Shared Reading (Acts 1–3), and Forensic Primary Evidence Analysis.
   - **Lesson 2:** Prior-Lesson Recall Retrieval, Act 4 Historiographical Debate, 2-Page Workbook Extended Writing Output, and Live Peer Exemplar Critique.
3. **The 2-Page Workbook Law:**
   Abolished 4-page booklets to eliminate cognitive split-attention. Every enquiry strictly occupies one double-page spread: **Verso (Left Page)** = Curated Evidence Launchpad & Retrieval; **Recto (Right Page)** = Structured Disciplinary Writing.
4. **Scheme of Work (SOW) Parity:**
   All classroom delivery phase timings are compiled directly into the printed departmental Scheme of Work.

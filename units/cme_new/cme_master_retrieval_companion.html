Tier 3 Academic & Disciplinary Vocabulary (High-Yield Examiner Lexicon)
Zionism [ZY-uh-niz-uhm]
The national movement advocating for the self-determination and sovereign statehood of the Jewish people in their ancestral homeland of Palestine.
Sovereignty [SOV-rin-tee]
Supreme independent authority and legitimate political power over a defined territory and population without foreign control.
Fedayeen [fed-ah-YEEN]
Arabic for "self-sacrificers"; armed Palestinian nationalist guerrillas who carried out cross-border raids and armed attacks into Israeli territory.
Pan-Arabism [pan-AIR-uh-biz-uhm]
The political ideology championed by Nasser promoting the unification of Arab countries across North Africa and the Middle East into a single bloc.
Pre-emptive Strike [pree-EMP-tiv]
A military attack initiated to disable or destroy an enemy’s offensive capability when an attack by that enemy is believed to be imminent.
Attrition [uh-TRISH-uhn]
A military strategy designed to wear down an opponent through continuous bombardment, economic disruption, and sustained personnel losses.
Demilitarised Zone (DMZ) [dee-MIL-i-tuh-ryzd]
An agreed geographic area where military forces, armaments, and military installations are prohibited by international treaty.
Intifada [in-tih-FAH-duh]
Arabic for "shaking off"; the spontaneous grassroots Palestinian uprising of civil disobedience, strikes, and stone-throwing beginning in December 1987.

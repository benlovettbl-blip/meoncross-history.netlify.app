A complete, 11-lesson modular scheme of work for Year 5 (Ages 9–10) comprising 10 Core Home Front & Rescue Enquiries plus 1 Literature & Rebuilding Lives Extension Enquiry. 1. Pedagogical Architecture & Curricular Foundation

National Curriculum (2014) Alignment

Directly addresses the statutory Key Stage 2 requirement: "A study of an aspect or theme in British history that extends pupils' chronological knowledge beyond 1066: a significant turning point in British history". Explores the impact of the Blitz, evacuation, civilian mobilization, technological innovation at Bletchley Park, and the moral duty of remembrance.

Holocaust Educational Trust (HET) Primary Guidelines

Fully aligned with A Guide for Primary School Teachers: (1) Pre-war diversity: Celebrates the richness of Jewish family life before 1939. (2) Gradual erosion of rights: Investigates how discriminatory laws stripped childhood rights step-by-step. (3) Agency & Dignity: Focuses on parental sacrifice, child resilience, and upstanders (Winton, Chadwick, Warriner). (4) Zero atrocity imagery: Uses only dignified archival photographs and authentic documents.

Teacher CPD & Free Primary Outreach: For complete primary teacher development courses, classroom artifact boxes, and educator guidance, visit the Holocaust Educational Trust at het.org.uk.

High-Challenge Disciplinary Reading & Writing

Every lesson pairs narrative historical reading with authentic primary sources (labeled strictly as Source A and Source B). Year 5 pupils develop key disciplinary skills: interrogating physical artifacts, chronological sequencing, comparing dual perspectives, and writing extended historical explanations on generous ruled handwriting lines.

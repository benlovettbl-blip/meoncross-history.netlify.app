## 🏛️ The Gold Standard Workbook Specification (The Benchmark)

Every perfected workbook strictly enforces the architecture established in **Year 7: Medieval England (`medieval_england`)**:

1. **Chromatic & Cover Discipline:**
   - **Pages 1 & 16 (Color Covers):** High-resolution primary artifact/plate (110–118mm height), museum accession metadata (e.g. UNESCO Memory of the World), 4 core features, 3-pillar decoding matrix, disciplinary significance statement, targeted hinge question, and calibrated enrollment/tracking grid.
   - **Pages 2–15 (Monochrome Interior):** Pure `#000000` text, borders, and ruled lines (eliminating all interior navy `#0f284e`, slate `#1e293b`, or gray backgrounds to guarantee crisp, zero-dithering reprographics).
2. **Handwriting Pitch & Page Budget Calibration:**
   - Ruled line pitch calibrated to **`~7.8mm–8.1mm`** across all response areas:
     * `single_12`: 21 lines (~7.9mm pitch)
     * `narrative_8` / single extended writing: 22 lines (~8.05mm pitch; whole-page format)
     * `split` (short drills only: 4m feature + 4m consequence): 4 + 18 lines (~8.0mm pitch)
     * `interpretations` (8m + 8m): 12 + 12 lines (~7.8mm pitch)
     * **Extended Question Sizing Principle:** When questions are short drills (e.g. 4m features + 4m consequence), two questions fit cleanly. But whenever a larger extended question is set (e.g. 8m narrative account, 8m importance, 12m essay), it MUST occupy the entire page alone to avoid condensed line pitch.
   - **100% `✅ OPTIMAL` Page Budgets:** Zero page overflows (0px) and zero dead void gaps (<30px footer space) verified via automated Puppeteer audits.
3. **Disciplinary Architecture & Stage Paragraph Indexing:**
   - **Verso (Left Page):** 5-Stage Chronological Inquiry Spine with 3 high-yield specification trigger bullets per stage, and prominent paragraph anchor badges (`[1.1]`–`[5.5]`) directly matching the master textbook and projected smartboard reading text.
   - **Recto (Right Page):** Rigorous disciplinary writing tasks with structured analytical stems and connectives.
   - **Zero Knowledge Giveaways:** Purely structural, content-free sentence stems and connectives (no factual hints or dates embedded in stems).
4. **Centrefold & Notes Spreads:**
   - **Pages 8–9 (Centrefold):** Panoramic Dual-Coding Living Timeline (lays 100% flat down the saddle-stitch staple line).
   - **Pages 14–15 (Notes Pages):** Authentic British exercise book layout with a **26mm margin rule** and **32 full-width handwriting lines** per page.
   - **Page 16 (Outside Back Cover):** KS3 Level Assessment Pathways (Emerging to Greater Depth), formal Progress Tracking Ledger, and 5 interactive QR code badges linking to online retrieval quizzes.

## 🎨 Dual-Pathway Living Timeline Standard & Retrospective Rollout Plan

### 1. Architectural Architecture & Pedagogical Grounding
Established and validated in **The Shoah (`the_shoah`)**, the **Dual-Pathway Living Timeline Standard** resolves a crucial classroom dilemma:
* **The Problem:** Compelling all pupils to sketch or draw historical events triggers anxiety, fine-motor fatigue, and task-avoidance among neurodiverse pupils or pupils who think in structural, analytical propositions.
* **The Solution (Choice A vs Choice B):**
  - **Choice A (Visual Dual-Code):** Pupils who process visually sketch symbolic representations, chronological icons, and dates.
  - **Choice B (3-Branch Concept Mind Map / Causal Web):** Pupils who process analytically draw a central concept node branching to 3 concrete historical evidence stems.
* **The 3 Touchpoints Enforced in Code:**
  1. **Centrefold Protocol Banner (P8–9 or P10–11):** Explicit instructions for both pathways.
  2. **Centrefold Milestone Canvas Footer:** `[ Choice A: Visual Dual-Code Icon • OR • Choice B: 3-Branch Concept Mind Map ]`.
  3. **Right-Page Writing Tasks (Bottom Timeline Mission Box):** Clear binary prompt (`Choose: [A] Dual-Code: ... • OR • [B] Mind Map: node "..." branching to ..., ... & ...`).
* **Ruled Lines Gap-Free Alignment:** Ruled writing lines attach directly underneath scaffolding boxes with a 1mm margin inside a unified `gap: 2px` flex container, absorbing the full page budget (22–23 lines extended writing, 8 + 14 lines split drills) with 0px overflow and 0 dead voids.

The First British Empire (1600–1783)

Key Events Timeline

1600 - Foundation of the East India Company

Queen Elizabeth I grants a royal charter to 218 London merchants, giving them a monopoly over English trade in the Indian Ocean.

1619 - First Enslaved Africans Arrive in Virginia

A privateer ship lands around twenty captive Angolans at Point Comfort, Virginia, marking the tragic start of chattel slavery in British North America.

1655 - English Conquest of Jamaica

Oliver Cromwell’s navy seizes Jamaica from Spain, rapidly transforming the island into the brutal, lucrative centre of the Caribbean sugar empire.

1739 - The British-Maroon Peace Treaty

Following years of mountain guerrilla warfare led by Queen Nanny, the British Crown is forced to recognise the sovereign independence of the Jamaican Maroons.

1757 - The Battle of Plassey & Conquest of Bengal

Robert Clive and the East India Company defeat Nawab Siraj ud-Daulah, seizing military control and vast tax revenues across Bengal.

1773 - The Boston Tea Party

American colonists disguised as Mohawk warriors dump 342 chests of British East India Company tea into Boston Harbour in defiance of imperial taxes.

1776 - The American Declaration of Independence

The Thirteen Colonies proclaim independence, asserting that all men are created equal while thousands of Black Loyalists fight for the British Crown in exchange for freedom.

Unit Conceptual Foundations • 4 Core Anchors
Feudalism: A rigid social hierarchy where the King owned all land, granting it to Norman tenants-in-chief in exchange for military service and oath-bound loyalty.
Chivalry: The medieval knightly religious, moral, and social code that governed aristocratic warfare, courage, piety, and honor on the battlefield.
Rule of Law: The constitutional principle established by Magna Carta (1215) that even the King is subject to the law of the land and cannot govern arbitrarily.
Serfdom: The legal status of medieval peasants (villeins) who were bound to their lord’s land, performing unpaid labour in return for a small plot and protection.

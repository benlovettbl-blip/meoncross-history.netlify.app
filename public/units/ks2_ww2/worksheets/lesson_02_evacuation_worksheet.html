Historical Reading • Operation Pied Piper

On Friday 1 September 1939—two days before war was declared—the British government launched Operation Pied Piper. Fearing that German bomber planes would immediately drop poison gas and bombs on industrial cities, authorities ordered the mass evacuation of vulnerable civilians.

In just three days, nearly 1.5 million schoolchildren and mothers were relocated from target cities like London, Manchester, and Liverpool to rural counties. Children gathered at their school gates carrying a single suitcase, their boxed gas mask, and a government identity label tied to their coat button.

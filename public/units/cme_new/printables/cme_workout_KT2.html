🧠 Section A: Rapid-Fire Knowledge Check (10 Facts) Cover, Recall, Self-Check
1. What major political entity was established at the January 1964 Arab League Summit in Cairo to represent the Palestinian national movement?
Ans: The Palestine Liberation Organization (PLO)
2. Which faction within the Palestinian movement, led by Yasser Arafat, advocated independent armed guerrilla struggle rather than reliance on regular Arab armies?
Ans: Fatah
3. What was the 'Water War' fought between Israel and Syria in the mid-1960s?
Ans: A series of military skirmishes over Syrian attempts to divert the headwaters of the River Jordan away from Israel's National Water Carrier
4. What major cross-border raid on 13 November 1966 saw the IDF attack a West Bank village in Jordanian territory following a Fatah landmine attack?
Ans: The Samu Raid
5. What dramatic air engagement on 7 April 1967 escalated border tensions between Israel and Syria to a fever pitch?
Ans: Israeli Mirage fighters shot down six Syrian MiG-21s and buzzed the skies over Damascus
6. What false intelligence report did the Soviet Union deliver to Egyptian officials in Cairo on 13 May 1967?
Ans: That Israel was massing 11 to 13 military brigades on its northern border to launch an invasion of Syria
7. What provocative step did President Nasser take on 16 May 1967 to demonstrate Egyptian solidarity with Syria?
Ans: He demanded that UN Secretary-General U Thant withdraw all UNEF peacekeepers from Sinai
8. What action by President Nasser on 22 May 1967 was treated by Israel as an unambiguous act of war (casus belli)?
Ans: The closure of the Straits of Tiran to all Israeli shipping and strategic goods
9. What pact signed in Cairo on 30 May 1967 convinced the Israeli cabinet that they were facing an imminent, coordinated three-front invasion?
Ans: A joint mutual defense pact signed between President Nasser and King Hussein of Jordan
10. Who was appointed Minister of Defense in Israel's emergency national unity government on 1 June 1967, electrifying public confidence?
Ans: General Moshe Dayan

🔑 Section C: Key Specification Terms (Non-Negotiable)
• UN Resolution 242 (1967): ‘Land for peace’ formula calling for Israeli withdrawal from occupied territories in exchange for recognized boundaries.
• The Conception: Israeli intelligence hubris assuming Egypt would never attack without long-range strike bombers to neutralize the IAF.
• Bar-Lev Line: A $300m chain of sand ramparts and concrete bunkers along the Suez Canal breached by Egyptian forces in October 1973.
• Operation Badr (1973): Coordinated Egyptian canal crossing on Yom Kippur using high-pressure water monitors and mobile SAM missile umbrellas.
⚠️ THE EXAMINER'S TRAP DOOR: Don't confuse the 1956 Suez Crisis with the 1967 Six Day War! Israel occupied the West Bank, Gaza, Golan Heights, Sinai, and East Jerusalem in June 1967, not 1956. In the 1973 Yom Kippur War, Egyptian assault troops breached the Bar-Lev Line with high-pressure water monitors, not conventional artillery!

2. Write a narrative account analysing the key events of the Six Day War (5–10 June 1967). (8 Marks)
Stage 1: Pre-emptive Airstrike (5 June)
Operation Focus wiped out 300 grounded Egyptian aircraft in 3 hours, granting Israel absolute aerial supremacy over all battlefields.
Stage 2: Fronts Encircled (5–7 June)
Jordanian artillery shelled West Jerusalem; Israeli paratroopers under Motta Gur captured East Jerusalem and the Western Wall on 7 June, securing the West Bank.
Stage 3: Golan Conquest & Ceasefire (9–10 June)
IDF armoured brigades scaled the Golan escarpment, seizing the plateau from Syria before accepting a UN ceasefire, quadrupling Israel’s size.

3. Explain the importance of the 1973 Yom Kippur War for Egyptian diplomatic leverage. (8 Marks)
Detailed Historical Facts (AO1) Causal Link to Outcome (AO2)
Egypt shattered the myth of Israeli invincibility by crossing the Suez Canal in Operation Badr, deploying Sagger missiles, and inflicting 2,600 Israeli casualties. Restoring Egyptian national pride and Arab military honour enabled Anwar Sadat to negotiate peace with Israel from a position of equal dignity, leading directly to the 1978 Camp David Accords.

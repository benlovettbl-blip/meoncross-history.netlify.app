⚡ 5 Quick-Fire Flash Checks:
1. What major political entity was established at the January 1964 Arab League Summit in Cairo to represent the Palestinian national movement?
Ans: The Palestine Liberation Organization (PLO)
2. Which faction within the Palestinian movement, led by Yasser Arafat, advocated independent armed guerrilla struggle rather than reliance on regular Arab armies?
Ans: Fatah
3. What was the 'Water War' fought between Israel and Syria in the mid-1960s?
Ans: A series of military skirmishes over Syrian attempts to divert the headwaters of the River Jordan away from Israel's National Water Carrier
4. What major cross-border raid on 13 November 1966 saw the IDF attack a West Bank village in Jordanian territory following a Fatah landmine attack?
Ans: The Samu Raid
5. What dramatic air engagement on 7 April 1967 escalated border tensions between Israel and Syria to a fever pitch?
Ans: Israeli Mirage fighters shot down six Syrian MiG-21s and buzzed the skies over Damascus

The 3 Exam Formulas

How to expand on spec points with 2–3 facts

1. 4-Mark Consequence (P-F-C)
Point: One consequence of [X] was...
Facts: Specifically, [Fact 1 & Fact 2]...
Link: This led directly to...
Model: King David Hotel → 91 killed → Attlee referred to UN in Feb 1947.
2. 8-Mark Narrative (T-P-C)
Trigger: The crisis began when...
Pivot: A decisive turning point occurred when...
Outcome: Consequently, the result was...
Model: 15 May invasion → June truce & Czech arms → Siege broken & 79% secured.
3. 8-Mark Importance (F-I-L)
Factor: [X] was important because...
Information: For example, [2–3 detailed facts]...
Link: Without this, [Y] would not have occurred.

Trap Doors & Challenge

Avoid common errors and test your recall

⚠️ EXAMINER TRAP:
Don't confuse the 1956 Suez Crisis with the 1967 Six Day War! Israel occupied the West Bank, Gaza, Golan Heights, Sinai, and East Jerusalem in June 1967, not 1956. In the 1973 Yom Kippur War, Egyptian assault troops breached the Bar-Lev Line with high-pressure water monitors, not conventional artillery!
⏱️ 4 Crucial Milestones:
1964: Cairo Conference establishes the PLO
1967: Six Day War: Israel captures Sinai, West Bank, Golan
1970: Black September: Jordanian Army expels PLO to Lebanon
1973: Yom Kippur War: Egyptian-Syrian surprise attack

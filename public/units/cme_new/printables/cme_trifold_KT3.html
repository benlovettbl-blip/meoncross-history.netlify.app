⚡ 5 Quick-Fire Flash Checks:
1. What diplomatic method did US Secretary of State Henry Kissinger pioneer in 1974–75, flying repeatedly between Middle Eastern capitals to broker disengagement?
Ans: 'Shuttle Diplomacy'
2. What major economic and infrastructural milestone occurred in June 1975 as a direct result of the Sinai disengagement agreements?
Ans: Egypt officially reopened the Suez Canal to international maritime shipping after eight years of closure
3. What political earthquake occurred in Israel in May 1977, ending 29 unbroken years of Labour Party dominance?
Ans: The right-wing Likud party led by former Irgun commander Menachem Begin won the national election (The 'Mahapakh')
4. What stunning announcement did President Anwar Sadat make to the Egyptian Parliament on 9 November 1977?
Ans: That he was prepared to travel to the ends of the earth, even to the Israeli Knesset, to plead the cause of peace
5. On what historic date did Anwar Sadat land at Ben Gurion Airport and address the Israeli Knesset in Jerusalem?
Ans: 19–20 November 1977

The 3 Exam Formulas

How to expand on spec points with 2–3 facts

1. 4-Mark Consequence (P-F-C)
Point: One consequence of [X] was...
Facts: Specifically, [Fact 1 & Fact 2]...
Link: This led directly to...
Model: King David Hotel → 91 killed → Attlee referred to UN in Feb 1947.
2. 8-Mark Narrative (T-P-C)
Trigger: The crisis began when...
Pivot: A decisive turning point occurred when...
Outcome: Consequently, the result was...
Model: 15 May invasion → June truce & Czech arms → Siege broken & 79% secured.
3. 8-Mark Importance (F-I-L)
Factor: [X] was important because...
Information: For example, [2–3 detailed facts]...
Link: Without this, [Y] would not have occurred.

Trap Doors & Challenge

Avoid common errors and test your recall

⚠️ EXAMINER TRAP:
In 'Importance' questions, you MUST explain why an event was important for the specific outcome named in the prompt (e.g. importance of Camp David FOR Egyptian-Israeli relations), not just write general knowledge about Jimmy Carter! And remember: Arafat renounced terrorism in Geneva in Dec 1988, which directly unlocked official US-PLO dialogue!

Master Chronology Domino Flowchart (1945–1995)

28 Causal Turning Points Across 50 Years of Middle Eastern Conflict and Diplomacy
1945
Post-WWII Jewish Insurgency Haganah, Irgun, and Lehi launch armed sabotage against British Mandate forces; illegal immigration surges.
Jul 1946
King David Hotel Bombing Irgun bombs British administrative HQ in Jerusalem, killing 91; shatters British domestic resolve.
Feb 1947
Britain Surrenders Mandate Attlee and Bevin announce Britain will surrender the Palestine Mandate to the United Nations.
Nov 1947
UN Resolution 181 Partition Plan UN votes 33-13 to partition Palestine (56% Jewish state, 43% Arab state; Jerusalem international).
Dec 1947
Outbreak of Inter-Communal Civil War Arab-Jewish fighting erupts over strategic highways, convoys, and towns across Mandatory Palestine.
Apr 1948
Deir Yassin & Plan Dalet Haganah secures strategic corridors; Irgun attacks Deir Yassin, triggering mass Palestinian flight.
14 May 1948
Declaration of the State of Israel David Ben-Gurion proclaims sovereign Jewish statehood as the British Mandate officially expires at midnight.
15 May 1948
Invasion by Five Arab States Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Iraq, and Lebanon invade; Israel mobilises the newly unified IDF.
Jun 1948
Czech Arms Shipments Arrive First UN truce allows Israel to import rifles, artillery, and Avia fighter aircraft from Czechoslovakia.
1949
Rhodes Armistices & The Green Line Fighting ends; Israel controls 78% of Palestine; Jordan annexes West Bank; Egypt holds Gaza.
1948–50
The Palestinian Nakba & UNRWA 700,000+ Palestinian refugees displaced; UNRWA established; Israel bars repatriation of refugees.
Jul 1950
Law of Return Passed Knesset grants automatic citizenship to all Jewish immigrants, doubling the national population by 1951.
Feb 1955
Israeli Gaza Reprisal Raid IDF paratroopers kill 38 Egyptian soldiers; humiliates Nasser and triggers the Czechoslovak Arms Deal.
Jul 1956
Nasser Nationalises Suez Canal Nasser seizes canal after US cancels Aswan Dam loans, outraging Britain and France.
Oct–Nov 1956
The Suez Crisis Protocol of Sèvres collusion; Israel invades Sinai; US financial threat forces British-French withdrawal.
Jan 1964
Cairo Summit & Founding of PLO Arab League plans Jordan River diversion; Palestine Liberation Organization (PLO) founded under Shukeiri.
1966–67
Samu Raid & Golan Air Clashes IDF reprisal raid on Samu and downing of 6 Syrian MiGs over Damascus sharply escalate border hostilities.
May 1967
Pre-War Escalation & Tiran Blockade Nasser expels UNEF, blocks Straits of Tiran, and moves 100,000 troops into Sinai; Jordan signs defence pact.
5–10 Jun 1967
The Six-Day War (100-Hour Blitz) Operation Focus destroys Arab air forces in 3 hours; Israel conquers Sinai, Gaza, West Bank, Golan Heights.
Nov 1967
UN Security Council Resolution 242 Establishes "Land for Peace" principle with disputed English wording regarding withdrawal from territories.
Sep 1970
Dawson’s Field & Black September PFLP hijacks 4 airliners; King Hussein crushes PLO in civil war, expelling Arafat’s forces to Lebanon.
Sep 1972
Munich Olympics Massacre Black September murders 11 Israeli athletes; Israel responds with covert Operation Wrath of God.
6–25 Oct 1973
The Yom Kippur War Egypt breaches Bar-Lev Line; Syria attacks Golan; US airlifts weapons; Sharon crosses Suez; ceasefire.
1973–75
Arab Oil Embargo & Shuttle Diplomacy OPEC quadruples crude oil prices; Kissinger brokers Sinai disengagement accords; Suez Canal reopens in 1975.
Sep 1978
Camp David Accords & 1979 Peace Treaty Carter, Sadat, and Begin agree peace; Sinai returned to Egypt; Egypt expelled from Arab League.
Jun 1982
Israeli Invasion of Lebanon Operation Peace for Galilee; siege of Beirut; Sabra & Shatila massacre; PLO evacuated to Tunis.
Dec 1987
First Intifada Erupts & Hamas Founded Jabalya incident sparks grassroots uprising; stone-throwing vs IDF; Hamas founded by Sheikh Yassin.
1993–95
The Oslo Accords & Rabin Assassination 1993 Oslo Accords signed at White House; 1994 Jordan peace; Oslo II signed; Rabin assassinated Nov 1995.

• Evidence (Precise Corroboration): Deploy 2 specific factual anchors (dates, treaty names, numbers, or key figures).
• Explanation (Causal Link): Explain exactly *how* and *why* the initial event produced this lasting result.
Golden Formula: Consequence Statement → 2 Factual Proofs → Causal "So What?" Link
Examiner Strategy & Golden Rules
• Single Consequence Rule: Only ONE consequence is credited. Writing two wastes valuable time with 0 extra marks.
• Avoid Narrative Drift: Do not explain *why* the event happened; focus 100% on the *aftermath and results*.
• Time Target: Strictly 5 minutes. Allocate 1 minute to plan and 4 minutes to write one precise PEEL paragraph.
Fatal Trap: Describing the event instead of its consequence caps your mark at Level 1 (1–2 marks)!

Practice Prompt: "Explain one consequence of the 1956 Suez Crisis for Great Britain's global superpower standing." [4 marks]
"One consequence of the Suez Crisis was the decisive collapse of Great Britain's standing as an independent global superpower. In November 1956, after Britain, France, and Israel secretly colluded under the Protocol of Sèvres to invade Egypt, US President Eisenhower threatened to collapse the British pound by blocking a critical $1.3 billion IMF loan and withholding oil supplies unless British troops withdrew immediately. Prime Minister Anthony Eden was forced into a humiliating and unconditional retreat within days. This exposed Britain's total economic and military subordination to the United States, effectively ending over two centuries of British imperial dominance in the Middle East."

• Broader Trajectory: Shift in diplomacy or military balance.

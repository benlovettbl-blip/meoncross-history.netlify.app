Round 3: Engine Room
1. 4-Mark Consequence Planning Grid: P-F-C Structure
Explain one consequence of Israel’s raid on Samu (1966) for tension between Israel and Jordan.
Point (Sentence 1) 2–3 Specific Historical Facts (AO1) Causal Link to Outcome (AO2)
One consequence was that it wrecked secret cooperative security relations between King Hussein of Jordan and Israel. On 13 November 1966, following a Fatah landmine, the IDF launched a massive punitive assault on the West Bank village of Samu, destroying 125 houses and killing 15 Jordanian soldiers. Riots erupted across Jordan demanding King Hussein’s overthrow, forcing Hussein to sign a mutual defence pact with Egypt in May 1967, ensuring Jordan would enter the Six Day War.
2. 8-Mark Importance Planning Grid: F-I-L Structure
Explain the importance of the PFLP airplane hijacks of 1970 for international attitudes towards the Palestine issue.
Core Factor Factual Information (Names, Dates, Treaties) Direct Link to Named Outcome
The Dawson’s Field hijacks forced the Palestinian national cause onto the front pages of world media, but alienated Western public opinion. In September 1970, George Habash’s PFLP hijacked four airliners, landing three in Dawson’s Field in Jordan, holding over 300 Western hostages, and blowing up empty aircraft on global TV. While it pressured Western powers to acknowledge that Middle East peace required addressing Palestinian identity, it triggered Black September and led Western governments to brand Palestinian guerrilla groups as terrorist organizations.
3. 8-Mark Narrative Storyboard Grid: 3-Stage Causal Arc
Write a narrative account analysing the actions of Nasser, the USSR, and the USA leading to the Six Day War (1967).
Stage 1: Catalyst / Outbreak Stage 2: Decisive Pivot Stage 3: Climax & Outcome
On 13 May, the USSR falsely warned Nasser that Israel was massing brigades on the Syrian border; to maintain his leadership, Nasser mobilized 100,000 troops in Sinai and expelled UN peacekeepers. On 22 May, Nasser closed the Straits of Tiran to Israeli shipping (an explicit Israeli casus belli) and signed a military pact with King Hussein of Jordan on 30 May, completing Israel’s encirclement. The USA refused to intervene militarily to open the Straits, signaling to Israeli Prime Minister Levi Eshkol and Moshe Dayan that Washington would not oppose an immediate pre-emptive strike.

🎯 AO2 (Historical Analysis): Sustained causal explanation. Explain consequence, importance, and causal linkages rather than narrative description.

🚫 Top 3 Fatal Examiner Traps to Avoid for Key Topic 2
• The 1967 vs 1973 Territorial Shift Israel conquered the Occupied Territories (Sinai, Gaza Strip, West Bank, East Jerusalem, Golan Heights) in the 1967 Six Day War, NOT in 1973. In 1973, borders remained essentially unchanged following the UN ceasefire.
• The Wording of UN Resolution 242 In UN Resolution 242 (1967), the English text intentionally called for Israeli withdrawal from "territories occupied" rather than "THE territories occupied". This deliberate ambiguity allowed Israel to claim it was not required to surrender all land.
• Israeli Intelligence and "The Conception" The catastrophic surprise of the 1973 Yom Kippur War was rooted in "The Conception" (Aman hubris) — the false Israeli assumption that Egypt would never attack without long-range strike aircraft capable of neutralizing Israeli airfields.

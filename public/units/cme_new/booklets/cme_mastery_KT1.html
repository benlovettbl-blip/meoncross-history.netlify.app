Round 3: Engine Room
1. 4-Mark Consequence Planning Grid: Explain one consequence of the formation of the United Arab Republic (UAR) in 1958.
Point (Sentence 1) 2–3 Specific Historical Facts (AO1) Causal Link to Outcome (AO2)
One consequence was that it encircled Israel with a unified Pan-Arab political union under President Nasser. Egypt and Syria merged into a single state in February 1958, placing Egyptian military commanders in control of Syria’s southern border with northern Israel. This heightened Israeli security fears of a coordinated two-front war and pushed Israel to seek closer defensive ties with the United States and France.
2. 8-Mark Importance Planning Grid: Explain the importance of the creation of the Israeli Defence Forces (IDF) for the aftermath of the 1948–49 war.
Core Factor Factual Information (Names, Dates, Treaties) Direct Link to Named Outcome
The IDF was important because it dissolved competing political militias into a unified, disciplined national army under state control. Established by David Ben-Gurion’s Order No. 4 on 26 May 1948, forcibly disbanding the Haganah, Irgun, and Lehi (culminating in the Altalena affair), and introducing universal conscription. This prevented factional civil war among Jewish groups, established firm civilian political control over the military, and created a permanent standing force capable of defending armistice borders.
3. 8-Mark Narrative Storyboard Grid: Write a narrative account analysing the key events leading to the end of the British Mandate in Palestine (1945–48).
Stage 1: Catalyst / Outbreak Stage 2: Decisive Pivot Stage 3: Climax & Outcome
Post-war Holocaust survivors sought entry; British White Paper strictly limited immigration to 1,500/month, triggering illegal Aliyah Bet ships (e.g. SS Exodus) and militant Jewish insurgency. Irgun and Lehi launched guerrilla warfare against British infrastructure, climaxing in the July 1946 King David Hotel bombing and the hanging of two British sergeants in 1947. Facing economic crisis at home and international outrage over turning back refugee ships, Britain announced in February 1947 it would surrender the Mandate, referring Palestine to the UN.

🚫 Top 3 Fatal Examiner Traps to Avoid for KT1
• Partition (1947) vs Armistice (1949) Do not confuse the 1947 UN Partition Plan (Res 181, which allocated 55% of Mandatory Palestine to a Jewish state) with the 1949 Armistice Green Line (which enclosed 79% following the 1948–49 War). Res 181 was never implemented as Arab leaders rejected it.
• The Turning Point of the 1948–49 War The decisive turning point of the 1948–49 War was NOT superior Israeli numbers at the outbreak, but the 4-week UN truce in June 1948. This allowed Israel to import Czech Avia S-199 fighters and rifles, while David Ben-Gurion unified all militias into the IDF.
• Suez 1956: Military Victory vs Political Defeat Britain and France achieved total military victory in capturing Port Said in November 1956, but suffered a catastrophic political humiliation when US President Eisenhower threatened financial sanctions, forcing an immediate withdrawal and elevating Nasser into a Pan-Arab hero.

⚠️ Top 3 Fatal Examiner Traps for Key Topic 1
• Partition (1947) vs Armistice (1949): Do not confuse the 1947 UN Partition Plan (Res 181, which allocated 55% of Palestine to a Jewish state) with the 1949 Armistice Green Line (which enclosed 79% following the 1948–49 War). Res 181 was never implemented as Arab leaders rejected it.
• The Turning Point of the 1948–49 War: The decisive turning point of the 1948–49 War was NOT superior Israeli numbers at the outbreak, but the 4-week UN truce in June 1948. This allowed Israel to import Czech Avia S-199 fighters and rifles, while David Ben-Gurion unified all militias into the IDF.
• Suez 1956: Military Victory vs Political Defeat: Britain and France achieved tactical military victory in capturing Port Said in November 1956, but suffered a catastrophic political defeat when US President Eisenhower threatened financial sanctions, forcing an immediate withdrawal and elevating Nasser into a Pan-Arab hero.

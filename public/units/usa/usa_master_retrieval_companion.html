28 Decisive Causal Turning Points • The Struggle for Civil Rights & Entanglement in Vietnam
May 1954
Brown v. Board of Education Supreme Court unanimously rules public school segregation inherently unequal, overturning 1896 Plessy doctrine.
Jul 1954
Geneva Accords on Indochina France withdraws after Dien Bien Phu defeat; Vietnam temporarily partitioned at 17th parallel with 1956 elections promised.
Aug 1955
Murder of Emmett Till 14-year-old Chicago boy abducted and murdered in Mississippi; open casket and all-white jury acquittal galvanise youth.
Dec 1955
Montgomery Bus Boycott Rosa Parks arrested; WPC launches 381-day municipal bus boycott led by 26-year-old Dr Martin Luther King Jr.
Nov 1956
Browder v. Gayle Supreme Court Ruling Federal courts strike down bus segregation laws under 14th Amendment, delivering decisive boycott victory.
Sep 1957
Little Rock Central High Crisis Eisenhower sends 1,200 soldiers of 101st Airborne to escort Little Rock Nine past mob and Governor Faubus.
Sep 1957
Civil Rights Act of 1957 First federal civil rights legislation since Reconstruction establishes Civil Rights Commission and Justice Dept division.
Feb 1960
Greensboro Lunch Counter Sit-Ins Four Black college students refuse to leave segregated Woolworth counter; sparks nationwide sit-in wave and SNCC founding.
May 1961
CORE Freedom Rides Interracial activists test interstate bus integration; endure Anniston bus bombing and Bull Connor Birmingham mob attacks.
Oct 1962
James Meredith Integrates Ole Miss JFK sends 20,000 troops and 300 federal marshals to overcome Governor Barnett and deadly campus riot at Oxford.
May 1963
SCLC Birmingham Campaign ("Project C") Bull Connor turns high-pressure firehoses and police attack dogs on children marchers; televised horror shocks nation.
Jun 1963
Thich Quang Duc Self-Immolation Buddhist monk burns himself to death in Saigon protesting Diem regime persecution; destroys US claims of democratic ally.
Aug 1963
March on Washington for Jobs & Freedom 250,000 gather at Lincoln Memorial; MLK delivers "I Have a Dream" speech, building unstoppable civil rights momentum.
Nov 1963
Overthrow of Ngo Dinh Diem South Vietnamese generals execute Diem with US acquiescence; leaves Saigon politically chaotic and unstable.
Jul 1964
Civil Rights Act of 1964 LBJ signs landmark law outlawing segregation in public facilities, ending job discrimination (EEOC), and cutting school funds.
Aug 1964
Gulf of Tonkin Resolution Congress grants LBJ blank cheque to take "all necessary measures" following alleged torpedo attacks on USS Maddox.
Mar 1965
Operation Rolling Thunder & Da Nang Three-year continuous bombing of North begins; first 3,500 US ground combat troops land at Da Nang air base.
Mar 1965
Selma to Montgomery Marches "Bloody Sunday" state trooper tear-gas attack at Edmund Pettus Bridge shocks 48 million TV viewers.
Aug 1965
Voting Rights Act of 1965 Outlaws literacy tests and deploys federal registrars; Southern Black voter registration surges from 7% to 60%.
Aug 1965
Watts Race Riots in Los Angeles Six-day riot leaves 34 dead; marks shift of civil rights crisis to northern urban poverty, housing, and police brutality.
Oct 1966
Black Panther Party Founded Huey Newton and Bobby Seale launch armed patrols in Oakland alongside Free Breakfast for Children programs.
Jan 1968
The Tet Offensive Vietcong strikes 100 cities and US Embassy; tactical defeat for North, but shatters US government Credibility Gap.
Mar 1968
My Lai Massacre & LBJ Abdication Charlie Company murders 500 civilians; LBJ announces on TV he will not seek re-election as presidency collapses.
Apr 1968
Assassination of Martin Luther King Jr. King shot dead in Memphis; violent riots erupt across 100 cities; Congress passes 1968 Fair Housing Act.
Nov 1969
Nixon's "Silent Majority" & Vietnamization Nixon announces gradual US troop withdrawals while appealing to patriotic middle-class Americans against protestors.
May 1970
Cambodian Invasion & Kent State Shootings Ohio National Guardsmen kill 4 student anti-war demonstrators; triggers nationwide student strike across 400 campuses.
Jan 1973
Paris Peace Accords Signed Ceasefire agreed; US POWs released; last American combat troops depart on 29 March, leaving 150,000 NVA in South.
Apr 1975
Spring Offensive & Fall of Saigon Congress cuts military aid; North Vietnamese tanks storm Presidential Palace; Operation Frequent Wind evacuates Americans.

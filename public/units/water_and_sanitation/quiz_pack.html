Unit Conceptual Foundations • 4 Core Anchors
Miasma Theory: The ancient belief that diseases like the plague and cholera were caused by inhaling foul-smelling air from rotting organic filth.
Laissez-Faire: The political philosophy that governments should not interfere in private enterprise or tax ratepayers for public health infrastructure.
Epidemiology (Dr Snow): The scientific study of disease patterns, pioneered in 1854 by Dr John Snow mapping cholera deaths to the Broad Street pump.
Germ Theory & 1875 Act: Louis Pasteur’s discovery of microscopic pathogens, which compelled parliament to pass the mandatory 1875 Public Health Act.

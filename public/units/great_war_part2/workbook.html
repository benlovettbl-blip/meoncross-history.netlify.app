Historian's Corner: The Outbreak of 1914: Fritz Fischer vs. Christopher Clark

Historians have fiercely debated who was responsible for the outbreak of war. Fritz Fischer (1961) argued that Germany bore sole responsibility, having planned a war for European domination. Conversely, Christopher Clark (2012) described the leaders of Europe as 'sleepwalkers', stumbling into war through mutual paranoia and failures of diplomacy, rather than a single calculated conspiracy.
